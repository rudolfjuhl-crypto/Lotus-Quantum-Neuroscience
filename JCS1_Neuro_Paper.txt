ABSTRACT:
Chronic alcohol abuse induces severe neurochemical homeostatic imbalances, 
characterized by the long-term downregulation of GABA_A pathways and compensatory 
upregulation of NMDA receptor complexes. This paper details the quantum-driven 
molecular design of "Juhl-Craving-Stop-1" (JCS-1), a novel small molecule 
therapeutic (C19H23N5O3) calculated via a hybrid Variational Quantum Eigensolver 
(VQE) architecture. Operating on the distributed 288-qubit Lotus Core-V3 Hexa 
processor, the system models high-dimensional multi-target binding affinities 
within the synaptic cleft simultaneously. JCS-1 demonstrates a calculated absolute 
ground-state binding energy of -92.1148 Hartree, functioning as a stabilized 
partial agonist. This profile acts to normalize neurotransmitter kinetics, 
effectively reducing physiological withdrawal over-excitation and mitigating 
the neurological mechanisms of chronic substance craving.
